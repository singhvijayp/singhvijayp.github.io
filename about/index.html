About

Hi — I'm Vijay Pratap Singh. I operate at the messy intersection where code meets paranoia (the healthy kind): threat modelling web apps, poking endpoints until they confess and chasing the next level up in Capture The Flag or a bug bounty hallway.

Today

I am sharpening my craft as a security researcher: manual testing, scripted checks and learning from tooling that mirrors how real engagements run. On the laptop next to that homework, I push BCA coursework at SEMCOM (CVM University, Anand)—databases, OOP logic, networking theory and deliberately wire those fundamentals back into offence and defence thinking.

How I grew into this

Earlier years in Vapi added discipline from science streams with Biology and Computer Science—not the most common pairing, yet it sharpened curiosity about systems inside and outside the silicon. Structured rooms on platforms like TryHackMe, community CTF chatter, lonely nights against broken PHP apps, early web pet projects—all of that compounded into confidence with Linux terminals, protocols, HTTP oddities and humility about how much remains unknown.

What I gravitate toward

Vulnerability discovery & mitigation for web-facing systems, reproducible exploits, clear write-ups instead of folklore, small tools that save minutes off repetitious work, mentoring-style notes for anyone still climbing the beginner wall. Languages and stacks shift; the reflex to verify claims and cite evidence sticks.

When the screens dim

I decompress with music-heavy walks, tinkering playlists, scribbling roadmap ideas across Notion remnants and occasionally reminding friends that patching is affection. If anything here resonates, the contact page points to sane ways to reach me—no interrogation chambers, merely email and links.
